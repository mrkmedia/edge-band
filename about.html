ABOUT DETAILS

MERAKI, the most trusted brand in India for Interior & Exterior Products, started in 2015 with the manufacturing of PVC Laminates as “the First Indian Manufacturer” and within a short span of time, MERAKI proved its success many times with the introduction of many innovative products with an excellent range. MERAKI, along with the industry leader in the PVC Laminates Industry, thereafter started new ventures of manufacturing multiple products like Acrylic Laminates, Interior Charcoal Louvers / Mouldings & Exterior Clads, becoming a one-stop solution for all your interior and exterior needs. Further, MERAKI is also manufacturing Edge Band Tapes, Mirror Profiles & MDF/HDHMR Boards by understanding the utmost needs of the hour of every smart customer and architect. Today, MERAKI is known for the world-class quality and service of its products. We bring to use highly efficient manufacturing units with special German technology in a world-class manufacturing infrastructure of 6.5 lakhs sq. ft. area, divided into multiple units where our employees carry out the manufacturing process in the most effective manner and eco-friendly environment. MERAKI LAMINATES INDUSTRIES PVT. LTD. is a widely sought-after name amongst manufacturers of building materials. We have been operational from Sonipat, Haryana since 2015.
